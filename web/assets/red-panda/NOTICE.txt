Source: user-provided RedPanda_Pet_App.zip, received 2026-11-06.
The original red_panda.glb is copied byte-for-byte (git blob SHA
8aeb766f7ceb3218cd1358e6adaa2769c6e84b2c). It contains Happy, Idle, Sleep,
TailWag, Tap and Walk clips; embedded textures and animation tracks are retained.

Verified local mesh repairs in pet-runtime.js apply only after the original
vertex count, joint names and index fingerprint match. Twenty-two whisker-tip
vertices (9741-9762), erroneously bound entirely to a front toe, inherit the
existing chin/jaw/lip weights of their whisker root (9763). Exactly 280 triangles
belonging to a dangling central under-tail fur flap and its attached tiny edges
are omitted from the draw index. This is a bounded repair of the loaded mesh;
the original GLB, all vertex positions, all four paw surfaces, textures and six
animation clips remain unchanged. No head, face or leg bone angles are rewritten.

pet-runtime.js also blends the original clip actions through the same frozen
Idle frame before and after reactions. Sleep remains in its original breathing
loop until interrupted; waking and stopping Walk settle back through Idle.
Walking screen travel uses only the active native gait, not transition motion.

The frame uses exact-origin parent messages, local asset loading, transparent
background, no visible floor/shadow/control/outline, no audio, 30fps rendering
and pixel ratio2.5-3. The orthographic camera has horizontal span1.50, ground at
its bottom border. Grounding reads the complete sole samples; during Sleep a
small whole-model local pitch (-2.65 degrees at full Sleep weight) aligns the
original unequal fore/hind paw planes. This display correction fades with the
Sleep blend and never changes a bone. Happy retains its native airborne motion.
Missing base-color textures fail quietly; embedded JPEG blob connections are
permitted by CSP. Hidden-page pause, static reduced-motion display, resource
cleanup and bounded same-origin commands are provided by the frame adapter.
The standalone HTML, preview images and platform examples are not included.
